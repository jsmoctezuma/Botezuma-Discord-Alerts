Botezuma Discord Alerts - Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Botezuma Discord Alerts handles data when used to notify a Discord community about new public TikTok videos from an authorized TikTok account.

1. Information the app may access

With permission from the TikTok account owner, the app may access limited TikTok information such as:

  • Basic profile information, such as display name, username, avatar, and profile link.
  • Recent public video information, such as video ID, title or description, cover image, duration, and public video link.

2. How the information is used

The information is used only to detect new public TikTok videos and post notification messages in a configured Discord server channel.

3. Data storage

The app may store minimal technical state locally, such as the last announced video ID and the last time the account was checked, to avoid duplicate Discord notifications.

4. Data sharing

The app does not sell personal data. Public video information may be displayed in Discord notification messages visible to members of the configured Discord server.

5. Access tokens

TikTok access tokens, if used, are stored as private server environment variables and are not intentionally exposed to Discord users or the public website.

6. Data deletion and access removal

The TikTok account owner may revoke access through TikTok account settings or by contacting the app operator. Local stored state can also be deleted by the app operator if needed.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect private configuration values and access tokens. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact: jsmoctezuma@lxlt.mx
