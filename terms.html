Botezuma Discord Alerts - Terms of Service

Effective date: May 24, 2026

These Terms of Service apply to Botezuma Discord Alerts, a private Discord bot integration operated by jsmoctezuma / Manco Squad.

Purpose

The app is used to notify a Discord community when new public TikTok content is posted by the authorized TikTok account.

Use of TikTok data

The app may access basic TikTok profile information and recent public TikTok videos only after authorization by the account owner.

Discord notifications

When a new public TikTok video is detected, the app may post a notification in a configured Discord server channel.
