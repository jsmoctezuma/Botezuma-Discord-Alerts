Botezuma Discord Alerts - Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Botezuma Discord Alerts handles data when notifying a Discord community about new public TikTok videos.

Information accessed

With permission, the app may access basic profile information and recent public video information from the authorized TikTok account.

How information is used

The information is used only to detect new public TikTok videos and post notification messages in a configured Discord server channel.

Data storage

The app may store minimal technical state, such as the last announced video ID, to avoid duplicate notifications.

Contact
