1. Purpose of the app

Botezuma Discord Alerts is used to notify a Discord community when new public content is posted by the authorized TikTok account. The app is intended for private community management and content announcement purposes.

2. Use of TikTok data

The app may access basic TikTok profile information and recent public TikTok videos from the authorized account, only after the account owner grants permission through TikTok authorization.

3. Discord notifications

When new public TikTok videos are detected, the app may post a notification in a configured Discord server channel. Notifications may include public video information such as title, description, cover image, video link, and publication details when available.
